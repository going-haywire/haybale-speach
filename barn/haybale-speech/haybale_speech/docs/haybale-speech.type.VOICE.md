# Voice

`haybale-speech:type:VOICE` · kind: type

A voice a text-to-speech model speaks with. Never saved with the graph.

## Details

- **wire_type**: `haywire-core:wire:DataWire`
- **default**: `{'value': {}}`
- **color**: `#d9a07a`

## Notes

A voice for Speak: its name, its language and the backend's voice data.

Pick Voice gives one of a model's own voices. A voice runs only on a model of its backend.
