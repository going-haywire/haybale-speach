# Whisper small

`haybale-speech:catalog:whisper-small` · kind: catalog

[Whisper](https://github.com/openai/whisper) `small` (244 M parameters, 99 languages), converted to CTranslate2 for [faster-whisper](https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper) by Systran. The weights are float16; Load STT Model runs them in int8 on the CPU and float16 on a GPU.
