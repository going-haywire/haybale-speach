# Kokoro 82M

`haybale-speech:catalog:kokoro-82m` · kind: catalog

[Kokoro](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) v1.0 (82 M parameters, 54 voices in 8 languages), exported to ONNX for [kokoro-onnx](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx). Speaks at 24 kHz. Voice names start with their language: `a` American and `b` British English, `e` Spanish, `f` French, `h` Hindi, `i` Italian, `j` Japanese, `p` Brazilian Portuguese, `z` Mandarin.
